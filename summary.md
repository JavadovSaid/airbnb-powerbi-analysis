# Executive Summary: HR Attrition & Retention Analytics

## Headline Finding
Voluntary employee attrition in the organization sits at **16.1%**, heavily concentrated among early-career professionals working regular overtime under lower income quartiles.

---

## 6 Key Findings (Backed by Data)
1. **Overall Attrition Rate:** Exactly **16.1%** of the workforce (237 out of 1,470 employees) left the company.
2. **Departmental Impact:** **Sales** experiences the highest attrition rate at **21.5%**, followed by Human Resources (19.0%) and Research & Development (13.8%).
3. **Critical Job Roles:** **Sales Representatives** exhibit an alarming **39.8%** attrition rate, followed by Laboratory Technicians at **23.9%**.
4. **The OverTime Factor:** Employees working OverTime (`Yes`) face an attrition rate of **30.5%**, compared to just **10.4%** for those with no overtime.
5. **Onboarding Vulnerability:** The **0–2 years** tenure band is the highest risk window, registering a **36.1%** attrition rate, which drops steeply to **7.5%** for employees with 10+ years of service.
6. **Compensation Gap:** Churned employees report a median monthly income ($\$4,787$) noticeably lower than retained staff ($\$5,196$).

---

## 3 Highest-Risk Employee Profiles
* **Profile A (The Overworked New Hire):** Employees in their first 2 years, working overtime in lower income tiers (Attrition exceeds **50%** in specific cross-segments).
* **Profile B (Frontline Sales):** Sales Representatives with high travel requirements and frequent overtime.
* **Profile C (Dissatisfied Support/Tech Roles):** Staff reporting low Job Satisfaction ($\text{Level } 1$) coupled with poor Work-Life Balance ($\text{Level } 1$, where attrition spikes over **30%**).

---

## 5 Prioritised HR Recommendations
1. **Targeted Onboarding Interventions:** Focus retention programs specifically on the 0–2 years tenure window. *Metric to track:* Reduction in first-year voluntary departures.
2. **Overtime & Burnout Capping:** Audit workloads in Sales and R&D to prevent chronic overtime abuse. *Metric to track:* Drop in overtime-driven resignation rates.
3. **Compensation Benchmarking for Sales Roles:** Review baseline salaries for Sales Representatives to align with market medians. *Metric to track:* Retention rate in the Sales department.
4. **Work-Life Balance Pulse Surveys:** Implement quarterly pulse checks for employees reporting low satisfaction scores ($\le 2$). *Metric to track:* Improvement in internal satisfaction scores.
5. **Manager Training in High-Risk Units:** Deploy targeted leadership coaching for managers overseeing high-turnover roles like Lab Technicians and Sales Reps. *Metric to track:* Team stability index over 12 months.

---

## Limitations of Analysis
1. **Synthetic Dataset:** This analysis is based on a structured IBM sample dataset and reflects simulated patterns rather than live internal company data.
2. **Absence of Time Dimension:** The dataset lacks a true longitudinal timeline, meaning we can measure static correlation but cannot track macroeconomic seasonality or historical shifts over years.
3. **Correlation vs. Causation:** Factors such as overtime or low income correlate strongly with attrition, but do not inherently prove direct causation without qualitative exit interview context.
